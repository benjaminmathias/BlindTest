# Surface brief — Blindtest (whole app)

- **Scope:** every existing screen and flow; the exact composition, DOM, class names and behavior stay. Visual identity only.
- **Mode:** Operate (party game; the visitor plays, doesn't read or decide).
- **Audience & scene:** francophone friends at a party, phones in hand plus a laptop, dim room; also solo players on phone or desktop.
- **Job:** start or join a room fast, hear the extract, guess the title under time pressure, see who leads, rematch.
- **Memorable moment:** the blank platter holding the secret track, the dot-matrix counter running down over it, then the cover arriving as the round ends.
- **Constraints:** all screens, copy, French voice, features and flows preserved; keyboard focus, aria live regions and reduced-motion preserved or improved; no framework; iTunes previews + Supabase unchanged; no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a physical analogue DJ console at a house party. It refuses both the flat dark dashboard it is replacing and the neon-on-black club cliché: every state is a switch, lamp or meter on a real faceplate, and the extract is a record on a platter.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite brushed-aluminium faceplates with walnut end-cheeks, cream screen-printed legends, incandescent amber indicator lamps, matte surfaces with inset bevels and 1px hairlines — no glow, no glass, no gradient text. Rubber fader caps, recessed input wells, a cream-dial VU meter with a black needle, a dot-matrix counter, a spinning jog platter. Roles: graphite ground, cream ink, signal orange-red as the record-armed primary, cue-cyan for the selected track, VU green correct, hazard red wrong. Type: Archivo for legends and UI, Doto for dot-matrix measurement. Recognizable with all content removed.

STORY: A first-time visitor understands "music machine" before reading a word: the platter is blank until the reveal, the counter runs down, the fader is the volume, the pad is record-arm. They name themselves, pick a game, and play; a room reads the same meters together.

FIRST VIEWPORT (home): a centred console faceplate with walnut end-cheeks; a screen-printed brand plate top-left and a lamp cluster top-right; a cream display receding to a VU-scale showing the best score; the settings as screen-printed switch legends over recessed select wells; the primary action as one large record-armed pad; below it a rail labelled for multiplayer, then pseudo and code wells and two pads.

FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7 — the analogue DJ console/mixer — in the audience's party world; seed key 4b2e0a52. Raised by the hand it beat: monumental dot-matrix countdown (anime crisis wall), mono measurement numerals and a live round log (phosphor terminal), the unrevealed cover as an undecoded plot (Saville sleeve), rounds that keep their mark (origami), one continuous scale across meters and progress (ocean dive), full keyboard operation (HyperCard).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
